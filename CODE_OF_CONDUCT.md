# Code of Conduct

Our Pledge: 
We aim to create a clear, safe, and positive environment by which anyone can contribute to the project free of harassment. 

NO harassment is tolerated and anyone is welcome to participate. New developer, experienced developer - we welcome everyone.

---

## Standards
We expect everyone to engage in a positive, constructive manner. We define positive behavior as includeing:
- Respectful language and communication
- Constructive feedback (we and to be productive, not negative)
- Openness to discussion and perspectives

We defines negative behavior as:
- Harassment and discrimination (both public and private)
- Personal attacks


If a contributor has issues with a project maintainer, they are welcome to contact another maintainer about the problem. Maintainers are expected to remain nuetral and provide support in the case of a community dispute.

---

## Enforcement

Our project maintainers hold the responsibility of enforcing these standards and clarifying them if needed. In the case that our standards miss and do not specifiy what the maintainers deem as "negative behavior", the action will be added to this document. From then out, it will be enforced and if the newly actioned "negative behavior" continues, then the individual will be removed from the project entirely.

When any "negative behavior" is actioned on, the individual will first be warned and then may be permanently banned from contributing. Depending on the severity of violation, a temporary ban may be enforced instead.

For more information:
- Warning: A private message, informing the contributor of what they did. This gives them the chance to correct the behavior.
- Temporary ban: A temporary removal from the project and community. The contributor will be allowed to continue participation after the ban timer is up.
- Permanent ban: The contributor will not be allowed to continue participation with the community, indefinetly.
